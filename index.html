Good to know

Before you convert.

Does NightPDF work offline?

After the app and its processing library are available in your browser cache, the core interface can work offline. The first visit requires a connection to load the PDF library.

Why does a complex PDF sometimes look unusual?

The converter uses a difference blend mode across the page. Most documents work well, but unusual transparency, embedded media, or pre-inverted graphics can produce unexpected colors.

Is this the same as changing my PDF reader theme?

No. NightPDF creates a new dark version of the file, so you can open it in any compatible PDF reader.
